# Roommate Hub

Roommate Hub is the private app the two of you use to share a schedule, reminders, notes, your Airbnb calendar, bills and files. Only the two household accounts can see anything in it. It works in any modern web browser, and you can add it to your phone's Home Screen so it opens like an app.

Open it at https://brogwalkerh.github.io/Roommate-hub/

## Getting started

### Create your account

Only the two household members can create an account. Each of you gets a one-time **Invite code** from whoever set up the app.

1. Open the app and choose **Create account**.
2. Enter your name, email, your **Invite code** and a password of at least 12 characters, then tap **Create account**.
3. The app emails you a confirmation link. Open it on the same device you signed up on, then sign in.

### Sign in

Enter your email and password, then tap **Sign in**. If you forget your password, tap **Forgot your password?** and open the reset link on this device.

### Invite your roommate

Go to **Settings → Your roommate**, enter their email and tap **Create invite**. You get a one-time code and a ready-made message with the link, their email and the code. Tap **Share…** (or **Copy message**) and send it however you like. The code is shown only once and works only with that email; if they lose it, tap **New code**. Once they've joined, this panel just says so.

### Set your name and color

Open **Settings** and find the **You** section.

- Set **Your name**, pick **Your color**, then tap **Save**. Your color marks your events, reminders and bills.
- Under **Household**, check the **Time zone**. Repeating reminders, recurring bills and morning notifications follow it.
- Under **Appearance**, pick **Match device**, **Light** or **Dark**. This choice applies to the device you are on.

## Install on your phone

Installing puts Roommate Hub on your Home Screen. On iPhone, notifications need it.

### iPhone and iPad

1. Open the app in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an up arrow).
3. Scroll down, tap **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.
4. Open Roommate Hub from its new icon on your Home Screen.

### Android

1. Open the app in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the menu (the three dots) and choose **Install app**. Some phones say **Add to Home screen** instead.
3. Confirm. The app opens in its own window from its icon.

### Turn on notifications

1. Open **Settings** and find the **Notifications** section.
2. Tap **Turn on notifications** and allow the prompt.
3. Tap **Send a test**. It should arrive within a few seconds.

Do this on each phone and laptop you want alerts on. iPhone and iPad need iOS 16.4 or later, and notifications work only after you open the app from its Home Screen icon.

## Using the app

On a phone, the tab bar at the bottom has **Home**, **Schedule**, **Reminders** and **Bills**. **Notes**, **Files** and **Settings** are under **More**. On a computer, the sidebar lists every section.

### Home

The **Home** page shows a card for each area:

- **Today** lists what is on the calendar today, except guest stays and blocked dates.
- **Due soon** shows overdue reminders and anything due in the next 24 hours. Tick a circle to finish one.
- **Guests** shows upcoming Airbnb stays. Until you connect the calendar, it links to **Settings**.
- **Bills** shows your balance and the bills due this week.
- **Pinned notes** and **Recent files** show your latest of each.

### Schedule

- Switch between **Month**, **Week** and **Agenda** at the top. Phones open on **Agenda**.
- Tap **Add event** to create one. You can also tap a day, or on a computer drag across days or times. Drag one of your events to move it, or drag its edge to change how long it is.
- Under **Whose event**, choose yourself, your roommate, or **Household** for things you both share.
- Guest stays are titled **Guest stay** and show check-in, check-out and the number of nights. When Airbnb includes a reservation link, the stay has an **Open reservation in Airbnb** button. Airbnb does not send guest names.
- **Blocked dates** (dates Airbnb marks unavailable) are hidden at first. Tap the **Blocked dates** chip above the calendar to show them.
- The chips above the calendar filter what you see: **Me**, your roommate's name, **Household**, **Guest stays**, **Blocked dates**, and each personal calendar.
- A personal calendar set to **Just “Busy”** shows only as "Busy" blocks, with no details.
- Tap **Import** to add a whole schedule from a file. See below.

### Import a schedule from a file

Got a work rota, class timetable or shift plan? Tap **Import** on the Schedule page.

1. Choose a file: a **PDF**, a **photo or screenshot**, or a **spreadsheet** (Excel, Numbers exported to Excel, OpenDocument, CSV). On a phone you can take a photo of a printed schedule.
2. **Name on the schedule** starts as your own name. On a team rota that lists everyone, only that person's row is read. Clear it to read the whole file. The app remembers what you typed last time.
3. If the schedule lists weekdays but no dates (a typical weekly rota), set **Week of** to the week it's for.
4. Tap **Read schedule**. Claude reads the file, which can take up to a minute.
5. Check the list. Every event can be renamed, moved to another day or time, switched to all day, or unticked. Events already on your calendar are marked **Already on the calendar** and start unticked, so re-importing an updated rota doesn't add doubles. Days off (OFF, leave, holiday) are listed too but start unticked; tick them if you want them on the calendar.
6. To fill in more than the one week or fortnight the file shows, tick **Repeat this schedule**, choose every **week** or **2 weeks**, and pick an end date. The button shows how many events that makes. Copies that already exist on the calendar are skipped.
7. Pick **Whose schedule is it?** and tap **Add events**.

Tips: a straight, well-lit photo reads best. A shift that ends after midnight (say 10 PM to 6 AM) is saved as ending the next morning. Claude's notes about anything it was unsure of appear at the top of the list. If something looks wrong, fix it in the list or edit the event afterwards.

### Reminders

- Type a reminder in the box at the top and press Enter. It is due at the next full hour (or 9:00 AM tomorrow if it is 9 PM or later), and it is for both of you.
- Tap **Add reminder** for more options: **Title**, **Notes (optional)**, **Due**, **Repeat** (**Every day**, **Every week**, **Every 2 weeks** or **Every month**, for chores like "Trash goes out Tuesday") and **Who’s it for?** (you, your roommate or **Both of you**).
- Tick a reminder's circle to finish it. The app shows **Marked done** with an **Undo** button. Finished reminders stay under **Done in the last 7 days**, where you can still **Undo** the latest one.
- Use **Show reminders for** (**All**, **Mine** or your roommate's name) to filter the list. **Mine** includes reminders for both of you. Overdue reminders are listed first, in red.

### Bills

- The **Balance** panel at the top says who owes whom, for example "Sam owes you $42.10". Tap **Settle up** to record a payment. The app only records it and does not move money, so pay with cash, Venmo or however you usually do.
- Tap **Add bill** to add an expense. Enter **What’s it for?**, **Amount**, **Category** and an optional **Due date**.
- **Split** can be **Equal**, **Exact amounts** or **Percent**. The app shows what is still left to assign, and when an amount does not divide evenly, it says who covers the extra cent.
- Tick **Already paid** to log a bill that is already paid, with **Paid by** and **Paid on**.
- On an unpaid bill, tap **Mark paid**, choose who paid, and confirm. The app offers **Undo** right after.
- Tap **Attach a photo or PDF** to add a receipt. It also appears in **Files**, in the **Receipts** folder.
- The tabs are **Upcoming** (unpaid bills, with overdue ones flagged), **History** (paid bills and payments, by month) and **Recurring**.
- **Recurring** holds monthly bills like rent and internet. Tap **Add recurring bill**, then set the amount, the day of the month it is due, and **Create the bill** (how many days before the due date it appears in **Upcoming**). **Usually paid by** fills in **Who paid?** when you mark it paid. Turn off **Add these bills automatically** to pause it.

### Notes

- Tap **New note**. Every note is shared by both of you.
- Notes save as you type. The line under the title says **Saving…**, then **Saved**. If a save fails, it says **Not saved, retrying**.
- Start a line with `- [ ]` to make a checklist item. Tap **Preview** to see the note formatted, then tap a box to tick it off.
- Tap **Pin note** to keep a note at the top of the list and on **Home**. Use **Search notes** to find any note by its title or text.
- If your roommate changes a note you are editing and your text conflicts with theirs, a box says who changed it and offers two buttons. **Keep my version** saves your text over theirs. **Load theirs** drops your unsaved edits. Changes from your roommate load on their own when you have nothing unsaved.
- Tap **Delete note** (the trash icon) to delete a note for both of you. This cannot be undone.

### Files

- Tap **Upload** and pick files. On a phone you can choose the camera, your photos or the Files app. On a computer you can also drop files onto the page.
- Each file can be up to 50 MB. Only the two of you can see them.
- Pick a folder when you upload, or choose **New folder…** to make one. The folder chips at the top filter the list. Files outside any folder show as **Unfiled**. A folder appears once it holds a file.
- Images preview right away. PDFs preview on a computer. On a phone, tap **Open PDF**.
- Open the actions menu on a file to **Open**, **Download**, **Rename**, **Move** or **Delete** it.
- Receipts attached to bills are kept in the **Receipts** folder.

## Connect your Airbnb calendar

Connecting your Airbnb calendar shows guest stays on **Schedule** and on the **Guests** card on **Home**. You only need to do this once.

### Find the export link in Airbnb

Do this on a computer or in the Airbnb app. Airbnb moves its menus around now and then, so the names may differ slightly.

1. Open **Calendar** and pick your listing.
2. Open **Availability**.
3. Choose **Connect calendars**. Airbnb may label it **Sync calendars** or **Connect to another website**.
4. Choose **Export calendar**, then copy the link. It ends in `.ics`.

### Add it to Roommate Hub

1. Open **Settings** and find **Connected calendars**.
2. Tap **Add Airbnb calendar**.
3. Enter a **Name**, such as the name of your listing.
4. Paste the link into **Calendar link**, then tap **Connect**. The app only accepts Airbnb links.

### How it updates

The app checks the link every 30 minutes. To check right away, tap **Sync now** in **Settings**. Airbnb updates its calendar on its own schedule, so a new booking can take a few hours to appear. If a calendar cannot sync, its row in **Settings** shows the problem in red.

Airbnb's feed has only dates, a reservation link and the last four digits of the guest's phone number. It has no guest names.

### Add a personal calendar

Personal calendars appear on **Schedule** in a color you choose. You can see them but not edit them there.

1. In **Settings**, under **Connected calendars**, tap **Add personal calendar**.
2. Set **Whose calendar** to you, your roommate or **Household**.
3. Set **What your roommate sees** to **Event details** or **Just “Busy”**.
4. Paste the link and tap **Connect**.

Where to find each link:

- **Google Calendar** (on a computer): open Settings, pick the calendar, and copy the **Secret address in iCal format**.
- **iCloud**: in the Calendar app, share the calendar as a **Public Calendar** and copy the link.
- **Outlook**: open Settings, then **Calendar**, **Shared calendars** and **Publish a calendar**, and copy the **ICS** link.

The link stays private. Once saved, the app cannot show it again, only replace it. To remove a calendar, open it in **Settings** and use the trash icon.

## Notifications

### What you get

- **Reminders** when they come due, usually within about five minutes of the time. A reminder that came due more than six hours ago is not sent, so an outage does not cause a burst of old alerts.
- **Bills** on their due day, from 9 AM in your household time zone. The notice shows the total and your share.
- **Guest checks in today** and **Guest checks out today**, from 9 AM on those days.

Each person chooses what to get, under **Settings**, **Notifications**, **Notify me about**. The two checkboxes are **Reminders when they’re due, and guest check-ins and check-outs** and **Bills on the day they’re due**. These choices apply to all of your devices.

### Troubleshooting

- **Notifications are blocked for this app.** Allow notifications for Roommate Hub in your browser or phone settings, then return to **Settings**.
- **iPhone says notifications need the Home Screen app.** Add it to your Home Screen as described above, then open it from that icon.
- **Nothing arrives.** Tap **Turn off on this device**, wait a moment, then tap **Turn on notifications** again and **Send a test**.
- **A new phone gets nothing.** Each device needs its own **Turn on notifications** step.

## Privacy

- Only the two household accounts can see anything in the app. The database's access rules enforce this, not just the app's screens.
- Calendar links are stored privately and cannot be viewed after saving. Files are kept in a private area and open through short-lived links.
- The source code is public on GitHub, but it has no personal data and no secret keys. The Supabase key in the code is public by design.
- When you import a schedule, the file is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to be read. The app doesn't keep the file, only the events you choose to add. Anthropic does not train its models on API data by default.

## Limits and costs

- **Cost:** $0 a month. The app runs on GitHub Pages (free for public code) and Supabase's free plan. If you outgrow the free plan, Supabase Pro costs $25 a month.
- **Limits:** 500 MB for the database, about 1 GB for files, and 50 MB per file.
- **Schedule import:** uses Claude Sonnet through the Anthropic API, billed to the maintainer's Anthropic account. A typical one-page schedule costs a few cents. Each person can import up to 30 files a day, and PDFs and spreadsheets can be up to 10 MB.
- **Pausing:** Supabase pauses free projects that go unused for seven days. Your data is kept. A daily GitHub Action called **Keep Supabase awake** pings the project to prevent the pause. If the project is paused anyway, restore it in the Supabase dashboard.
- **GitHub:** GitHub turns off scheduled workflows after 60 days with no activity in the repository. When that happens, GitHub emails the repository owner a one-click link to turn them back on.

## For the maintainer

The original design and decisions are in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md). Working conventions are in [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md). Where they differ from the code, the code is right.

### Stack

React, TypeScript, Vite and Tailwind CSS on the front end, with FullCalendar for the calendar, TanStack Query for data and React Router for pages. The backend is Supabase: Postgres with row-level security, Auth, Storage, Realtime and Edge Functions (Deno). The app installs as a PWA through vite-plugin-pwa, and its service worker is `src/sw.ts`.

### Commands

```
npm install      # Node 22, the version CI uses
npm run dev      # local dev server; needs .env.local (below)
npm run check    # lint (oxlint), typecheck and unit tests (vitest); must pass before every commit
npm run build    # production build into dist/
```

`.env.local` (git-ignored) holds:

```
VITE_SUPABASE_URL=https://<project-ref>.supabase.co
VITE_SUPABASE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY=<publishable key>
VITE_VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY=<VAPID public key, for push>
```

### Deploy

Push to `main`. The **Check and deploy** workflow (`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`) runs the checks and the build, then publishes `dist/` to the `gh-pages` branch, which GitHub Pages serves (Settings, Pages, Source: Deploy from a branch, `gh-pages`).

The deploy workflow does not pass `VITE_` variables. Before the first deploy, put your project's URL, publishable key and VAPID public key in the `PRODUCTION` block of `src/lib/config.ts`. These values are public by design.

### Where things live

- `src/features/<section>/`: one folder per section (`home`, `schedule`, `reminders`, `bills`, `notes`, `files`, `settings`), each with a `<Section>Page.tsx` and its hooks.
- `src/layout/nav.ts`: the list of sections, and which ones appear in the phone tab bar.
- `src/auth/`: sign-in and password screens. `src/household/`: profiles and household settings.
- `src/components/ui/`: shared buttons, dialogs and form fields.
- `src/lib/`: the Supabase client, `config.ts`, generated database types, and money and date helpers.
- `supabase/migrations/`: SQL, applied in filename order. Add a new file for each schema change.
- `supabase/functions/`: `sync-calendars` (Airbnb and personal calendars) and `send-notifications` (push). Shared code is in `_shared/`.

### Add a roommate

Normally you do this in the app: **Settings → Your roommate → Create invite** (see the user guide above). It calls the `create_invite` database function, which stores only a bcrypt hash of the code in `public.allowed_emails` and refuses once the household has two members.

An account can be created only with an email listed in `public.allowed_emails` together with that email's one-time invite code. This stops anyone who guesses a household email from registering it first. If you ever need to do it by hand, run this in the Supabase SQL editor, with the address in lowercase and a long random code you then share privately:

```sql
insert into public.allowed_emails (email, code_hash)
values ('name@example.com', extensions.crypt('the-invite-code', extensions.gen_salt('bf')))
on conflict (email) do update set code_hash = excluded.code_hash;
```

The code is cleared once it's used. Run the same statement again to issue a new one. Once you both have accounts, you can also turn off **Allow new users to sign up** under **Authentication** in the Supabase dashboard.

### Scheduled jobs and secrets

- `pg_cron` runs `sync-calendars` every 30 minutes, `send-notifications` every 5 minutes, and `generate_recurring_bills()` hourly at minute 7.
- The header of `supabase/migrations/20261015000003_jobs.sql` explains the two Vault secrets the jobs need. The `cron_secret` in Vault must match the `CRON_SECRET` Edge Function secret. Push also needs the Edge Function secrets `VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY` and `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY`, and optionally `VAPID_SUBJECT`. Schedule import needs `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` (create one at console.anthropic.com, and set a monthly spend limit there). If the key isn't scoped to a workspace, also set `ANTHROPIC_WORKSPACE_ID` (the `wrkspc_...` ID from the Console's workspace settings). Each secret can instead live in Vault under its lowercase name; Edge Function secrets win.
- `parse-schedule` reads files with `claude-sonnet-5-5` using structured output, then cleans up the answer (`supabase/functions/_shared/schedule.ts`). Nothing is saved until the person confirms in the review list. The `ai_usage` table counts reads for the daily limit.
- In Supabase, under **Authentication**, **URL Configuration**, set the **Site URL** to the app's address and list exactly that address under **Redirect URLs** (no wildcards). Otherwise, sign-up confirmation links will not lead back to the app.
- Under **Authentication**, keep **Confirm email** on and set the minimum password length to 12.

Keep secrets out of the repository. Calendar links and keys belong in the database or the Supabase dashboard, never in code.

### Conventions

Read `CLAUDE.md` before changing code. The main rules: colors come only from the semantic theme tokens (no raw hex and no `dark:` variants), money is stored as integer cents, all-day dates are date strings parsed as local dates, and pure logic such as money splits and date math has unit tests.
